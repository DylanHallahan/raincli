## What changed (all 18 acceptance criteria met)
- **Live thread:**
  - **Updates:** while the window is visible, one `/app/live` call every 5 s brings new messages, delivery-state changes, header presence, the sender warning and the conversation list (only when it changed). Polling stops while the window is hidden, and backs off to 30 s on network errors.
  - **Robustness:** late commits are caught by a 30-second overlap merged by message id, and keyboard focus and scroll survive updates.
- **Read only when seen:**
  - **In app mode,** a message to you counts as read only when the window has focus and the message (or half the screen) has been visible. Opening or polling never marks anything read. §19.2 records this change.
  - **Scope:** the read call never touches messages addressed to machines.
  - **The website** keeps its rule until D4.
- **Header:**
  - **Agent conversations** show the reachability chip (Instant or Next turn) and a presence word: Ready, Busy or Offline. The Busy qualifiers are also spoken to screen readers.
  - **Offline rule:** after 2 minutes without a report the presence shows Offline, with no Instant chip.
  - **Never claimed:** typing, or work on a particular message.
  - **The kind pill** is gone.
- **Names:** *Owner · agent* and *Owner · inbox*, worked out on the server, with the handle on the header's second line.
- **The "not connected" warning,** in app mode **and** on the website:
  - **Cases:** never connected, not connected for 24 h, or not picking up for 24 h.
  - **Format:** one warning at a time, with a date only.
  - **Where it shows:** to senders on compose and in the header, and to the owner on their Inbox.
  - **Display only:** delivery is unchanged.
- **Thread:**
  - the delivery trail per recipient kind (stored → received → submitted → replied for agents), with held, uncertain and rejected shown with their reasons;
  - local-time step tooltips, one screen-reader phrase, and a Details disclosure;
  - no state on incoming messages;
  - the meta line, and Reply on hover and focus;
  - an attachment card with a short hash;
  - a one-row composer that grows to 8 lines, with Ctrl+Enter;
  - only your own avatars accented;
  - motion that respects reduced motion.
- **Sidebar:**
  - a Team section with presence dots (text labels and tooltips; click to message);
  - Today, Yesterday and Earlier groups in local time;
  - a three-step first-run Inbox.
- **Minimum width:** the app-mode breakpoint now sits below the 760×520 window minimum, so the conversation list always shows.
- **Migration `0009`:** a partial index for "oldest unacknowledged message per machine".

## Answers to the brief's open questions
1. **The poll cost for 5–8 windows.**
   - **The design:** yes, one call returns the thread delta, states, header, warning and list (only when changed).
   - **Measured on real PostgreSQL:** 8 windows give a median **29.6 ms** (p95 32.8 ms) and 26 queries per poll, constant in thread length. That is about 47 ms of server time per second at 8 windows every 5 s.
   - **On a very long thread** (20,000 messages): a median 79.6 ms. The remaining cost is the list's per-conversation counts. This is acceptable for the team today, with a follow-up if threads grow (keep the counts out of the list digest, or maintain them).
2. **Presence without a per-message query.** Yes: two indexed reads per conversation (the machine's presence row and its live agent rows), and one events query for the whole thread.
3. **An index for 9b.** Yes. Migration `0009` changes `ix_messages_recipient_pending` to `(recipient_agent_id, seq) WHERE acked_at IS NULL`, so "oldest unacknowledged" is a single index lookup.
   - **Note:** the rule uses "oldest unacknowledged" (§19.6), so a machine that picks messages up but holds them doesn't warn, which is correct.

## Release notes (draft)
- **Live updates:** the app window updates on its own: new messages, delivery states and who's available, every few seconds while it's open.
- **Who can be reached:** conversations with agents show whether they can be reached now (Instant or Next turn) and whether they're Ready, Busy or Offline.
- **Names:** conversations are named after people: *Jon · api-agent*.
- **Delivery trail:** your messages show where they are, and why, if they're held.
- **A warning** when you message a machine that hasn't connected or picked up messages for a day.
- **A tidier window:**
  - a team list with presence;
  - Today, Yesterday and Earlier groups;
  - a first-run guide;
  - a one-line composer (Ctrl+Enter to send);
  - the list always visible, even at the smallest window size.
- **Read state in the app:** messages count as read only once you've actually seen them.
