## The model
1. **Addressing:** everyone addresses people, never agents. A person, or one of their agents, sends to "Jon", by email or by Jon's name in the team.
2. **Inboxes:** each person picks, per team, one agent of theirs that receives their teammates' messages: their **inbox**. Until they pick one, messages wait for them in the app.
3. **Replies** go back to the agent that asked, while it is live. Otherwise they go to the asker's inbox.
4. **What teammates see:** people and their availability ("Ready · Instant", "Busy · Next turn", "Offline", "No inbox yet"). Your machines and agents are shown to you.
5. **Existing conversations** are merged into person-to-person conversations. Conversations between your own machines become one private, archived "Your agents" conversation.

## The brief's open questions
1. **Replies to an asking agent:** a returning reply is an ordinary named-agent message for the sender's machine. v0.5.0 and later connectors deliver it with no new client logic. The server decides the target once, at send time; the fallback to the inbox is also decided there.
2. **The privacy scan's route list:** kept with the review notes. The scans now enumerate every API and app route and fail on any route they don't cover.
3. **Hosted inbox picking from presence data alone:** yes. A person's deliverable agents across their machines in a team come from the presence reports their machines already send.
4. **How long the migration takes on production:** about a third of a second (0.26–0.32 s) on a production-sized copy. The service is stopped for the install anyway.

## Rollout (main)
**Day 0, server:**
1. Take a backup.
2. Run the migration dry run on a restored copy of that backup (`upgrade`, check the counts, `downgrade 0009`, check it's exact).
3. Stop the service and install the release. `install.sh` backs up, migrates and starts it (see `docs/raincli-deploy.md`, "Upgrading to the v0.6 server").
4. **Live checks:**
   - the privacy scan as a team member;
   - a person-addressed send;
   - a `no_inbox` hold for someone with no inbox.
5. Push the branch. Run CI, including the Windows e2e: picking an inbox in the real window, person delivery, a reply returning to the asking agent, and a v0.5.1 client receiving.

**Then, the client:**
6. Release v0.6.0 and set it as the team's client target.

**Days 1–3:**
- Check `no_inbox` holds daily, and send one reminder (with the user's approval) to anyone who hasn't picked an inbox.
- Check that replies arrive back at the asking agent.

**Rollback:** run the migration's downgrade to 0009 before switching back (`docs/raincli-deploy.md`, "Rolling back the v0.6 server"). v0.5.x clients are unaffected either way.

## Release notes (v0.6)
- **You message people now.**
  - Send to a teammate by email or by their name in your team: `raincli send jon@example.com`, or pick them in the app.
  - Replies come back to the agent that asked.
  - Addressing a machine or an agent is refused, with the person to use instead.
- **Pick your inbox (everyone, once per team).** It's the agent that receives your teammates' messages. Until you pick one, messages to you wait in the app, and your senders see "waiting for *you* to pick an inbox". Pick it in any of three places:
  - the Inbox banner in the app or on the website (any client version);
  - This computer in the app (v0.6.0);
  - `raincli inbox set <agent>` (v0.6.0).

  When you pick, the messages waiting for you are delivered there too, unless you untick that.
- **People:** the People page shows your teammates with "Ready · Instant", "Busy · Next turn", "Offline" or "No inbox yet". Your machines and agents are shown only to you, on This computer and the website's Machines page. Team owners also see every team machine's client version and last connection.
- **Conversations:**
  - your conversations with each teammate are now one conversation;
  - messages between your own machines are kept in an archived "Your agents" conversation;
  - your agents' escalations and reports to you appear in "From your agents";
  - old links still work.
- **Client v0.6.0:**
  - `raincli people`;
  - `raincli inbox show`, `raincli inbox set` and `raincli inbox clear`;
  - `raincli send <person>`;
  - `raincli agents` now lists people;
  - the agent frame says who sent a message and that you received it as someone's inbox;
  - notifications only for what needs you ("Notify me about every message" turns on the rest).
- **For pilot users:** pick your inbox after the update. Machines on a client older than v0.5.0 need updating first.

## Known limits
- **Inboxes are per team.** A person in several teams picks one in each team. Messages never cross teams.
- **A small window when changing inbox on the same machine:** a message that machine's connector has fetched but not yet acknowledged keeps the agent it was fetched for (§21.5).
- **The message sequence number is global.** It orders messages and serves as the poll cursor.
- **`name_taken`:** adding a machine with a name already used in the team is refused with "name taken". Choosing your own machine's name needs that answer.
- **Clients older than v0.5.0** need an update to receive messages under §21.
